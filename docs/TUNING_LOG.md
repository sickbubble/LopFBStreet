> `ball.json`, so every row below holds on the web too. **A row made on the

## How to tune

Run the game. Scene dock → **Remote** tab → select the node → expand the group
in the Inspector. Edits apply live, no restart.

**Which node holds the number matters, and half of them are no longer on the
ball.** Since the body plays the ball (S16–S26) a parameter typed on the wrong
node does nothing and reads as a dead value:

| Node | Groups | Whose numbers |
|---|---|---|
| `Ball` → `Tuning` | Touch, Carry Levels, Bounce, Stall, Launch, Possession, Reception, Verb Bands, Body | **The ball's** — the touch, the four levels, the flight, the trap, the launch |
| `Player` | Body, Strike, Stride, Pose, Reaction, Follow | **The body's** — the limb reaches, the foot's strike path, the stride clock, the part strikes and poses, the reaction springs, the follow |
| `Animator` | — | The swing fractions measured from the clips (`WalkRightSwingAt`, `RunRightSwingAt`, `SprintRightSwingAt`) |

The rule behind the split: the ball's numbers are the game's and never change
with who is carrying it; the body's belong to the *player*, because co-op will
have a body per player.

Change **one parameter at a time**. Two at once and the result teaches nothing.

> **All of S16–S26 has landed** (M0.C2–M0.C19, M0.D1–D14, through `b3cee53`),
> and every row below is written against the build as it stands. The two
> "coming with" notes that stood here — for S16/S17 and for S23–S26 — are
> retired, because the rows they warned about have been rewritten. What they
> changed, kept here so an old complaint is not re-diagnosed against the old
> model: **reach is the body's** (S16) and `KeepUpReach` is only the outer edge
> of the cap ramp; **the foot keeps the ball up off the instep** (S17), so
> "bounces in place" is now a miss and not the design; **the body follows the
> ball** (S23), so "runs away when I stop", "lands behind me when I turn" and
> "the sprint touch gets away" are the follow's numbers, never `FlightEase` /
> `TouchLeadPerSpeed` / `FootReach`; **every level keeps up** (S25), so
> `BreakRadius` is 0 at thigh, chest and head until the stalls return (M0.D16),
> and `ThighTouchHeight` is 0.85; **the first touch** (S24) and **the part
> strikes** (S26) have rows of their own below.
>
> **Still not built, so still not tunable:** the stalls at thigh, chest and head
> (M0.D16) and the reaction layer's scene half (M0.C18, C20). A complaint about
> holding a ball on the chest is *not yet built*, not a tuning miss.

### The order

Not arbitrary — each one changes how the next feels.

| | Parameter | Default | Question it answers |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | `StallCoupling` | 0.08 | **The difficulty dial of the whole stall.** How hard your acceleration throws a held ball off the body. At 0.08 a steady run costs nothing, a sprint start holds at every level, and a full reversal at sprint pace loses a head stall but not a chest one. Raise it if you can do anything you like while carrying on your chest; lower it if you cannot keep the ball at all. |
| 0a | `StallRestore` / `StallDamping` | 6.0 / 4.0 | How weakly a held ball is drawn back to its rest point, and how fast the wobble settles. **Raise `StallRestore` far and the invisible string is back** — the tell is that you can cut and sprint at head level and never drop it. | 0b | Level `BreakRadius`es: thigh / chest / head | **0 / 0 / 0 since S25 (M0.D3)** — the stall values were 0.35 / 0.25 / 0.15 and return with the stalls (M0.D16) | **Zero is a keep-up, not a loose stall**: every level keeps the ball up until M0.D16, so the stall rows above (0, 0a) and this one are parked. Foot is 0 and **must stay 0** — that is what makes the foot kept up rather than stalled (a bounce-dribble off the ground until S17, instep keep-ups after). |

### Symptom → first suspect

| Symptom | Suspect |
|---|---|
| **The ball follows me / chases me** | Level `Correction` too high — that is the one positional pull left in the model, and it is deliberate and tunable. Nothing acts on the ball positionally *between* touches; if it moves toward you while airborne, that is a `BounceSolver` bug and not a value. Said "impossible since S8" here for a while; it was not, and the pull was in the touch. |
| The ball still bounces at chest/head instead of being held | **By design since S25 (2026-09-22):** every level keeps up until the stalls return at M0.D16. After that: a level's `BreakRadius` is 0, which turns the stall off for it. |
| I can cut and sprint at head level and never drop it | `StallCoupling` too low or `StallRestore` too high. This is the string tell — target a break every 10-20 s of ordinary head-level play, and treat "I never lost it" as a failure report, not a success. |
| I lose the ball constantly, I can barely move | `StallCoupling` too high. Check it against a *steady* run first: constant velocity is free by design, so if a straight sprint loses it, something is wrong beyond tuning. |
| The held ball looks welded to the capsule | `MicroMotion` too low for that level. Pure legibility, no effect on the rules. |
| I can sprint with the ball on my head | `CarryRadius` too small, so a small pop escapes it. This was a rule bug before S9 and it should not be reachable by tuning at all — if it is, say so rather than tuning around it. |
| The ball is tidy at every level, the levels feel the same | `Correction`s or `LeadFactor`s too close together. The spread is what makes high precise and low loose. |
| Ball runs away when I stop mid-flight | **Since S23 (M0.D6) the body carries on to the ball and stops on the next touch** — a stop costs one flight. If the ball is still lost: the HUD's plan line lacks `FOLLOW` (the ball was not under control — S24), or a bug. Not `FlightEase`, not `FootReach` |
| Ball lands short / behind me when I turn | **Since S23 a turn takes one touch** and the body faces the ball meanwhile. If it still lands behind: `FlightEase` too high (it is chasing your new intent), then a bug |
| Sprint touch gets away from me | **Since S23 the body runs onto it at up to `SprintSpeed`.** If it still gets away, the knock-on is beyond the sprint — `TouchLeadPerSpeed` too high — or the follow was off (no `FOLLOW` on the plan line) |
| I cannot lose the ball at all, whatever I do | Since S23, expected inside the follow. The dial is the follow's top speed (`SprintSpeed`) and, later, M1's obstacles — never a new rule |
| Sprint touch lands under my feet, I trip on it | `TouchLeadPerSpeed` too low |
| I miss touches I feel I should have made | `KeepUpReach` too low |
| The ball gets kept up from absurdly far away | `KeepUpReach` too high (max lead is 1.05 m, so below that is always wrong) |
| I never press the bounce button | Level caps too high — carrying is as good as pressing, so pressing has no point |
| Carrying feels like a punishment | Level caps too low, or `LevelSlowTime` too short (a jolt) |
| I cannot tell which level a hold will give me | `BounceChargeTime` too short. Lengthen before cutting a level |
| The keep-up rhythm is frantic / sluggish | Level apexes too low / too high (period is 2v/g) |
| A full bounce flickers to FLIGHT at the top | `HeadApex` or `BounceMaxApex` too close to `PossessionRadius` (3D, from the origin) |
| Speed lurches every time the ball lands | `LevelSlowTime` too short. The contact steps of a touch should never register as a level change; if they do, that is a bug in `BounceSolver.SpeedFactor`, not a tuning value |
| The ball pops early when I jump | Fixed by S12: heights are measured from `PlayerMotor.Feet`, the last grounded height. If it still happens, that is a bug in `Feet`, not a value |
| The ball sits in front of the body instead of on it, at chest or head | *That level's* hold offset — `ChestHoldOffset` or `HeadHoldOffset`, each its own value since the per-level split. Watch `TouchPoints`: the spheres are exactly where the solver holds the ball. Chest can come down to the 0.18 floor (`ChestDepth` + ball); head has no floor and 0.08 puts it on the hairline. If a chest ball is *inside* the mesh instead, `ChestDepth` 0.12 is an estimate — measure it. Never move all four together to fix one |
| **"the values are for a cylinder shape"** | A complaint about the **volume**, not about a distance, and the two have different fixes. `InReach` (`BounceSolver.cs:279`) is `flat <= KeepUpReach` over a Y-discarded distance: a cylinder of infinite height, with no facing term. **Do not read it as "`KeepUpReach` is too big" and shrink it** — that pays for a wrong shape with lost touches. The forward-arc facing term is S13 / M0.12r, code. The same word covers a second, real cylinder: `HoldOffset`'s 0.46 floor is the 0.35 capsule radius, which is the same in every direction where a torso is not |
| The landing ring sits well forward of the feet while the ball is *held* | Not a bug and not the predictor. A stalled ball has ~zero velocity at ~1.05 m, so it lands in ~0.44 s, inside `MaxPredictSeconds` 4.0 — the ring is drawn, directly under the held ball. **So its distance from the feet reads out that level's hold offset plus the balance offset**, and it is the cheapest way to measure a hold-offset change on screen |
| The ball is invisible during a chest stall | The model has swallowed it: same symptom as the very first F5 on the capsule. `ChestHoldOffset` is the knob, and the boot floor should have caught it — if it did not, `ChestDepth` is under-estimated. **Never fix it by sliding `Character` forward** — that decouples the mesh from the capsule and every height is then measured from a body that is not where the code thinks it is |
| The ball bounces in place when I stand still at foot level | **Until S17:** designed (a street bounce-dribble off the ground). **After S17:** the foot keeps it up off the instep, so a standing ball bouncing on the ground is a miss (reach) or a bug in the lift, never the design |
| *(from S16/S17)* The wrong foot plays it | `SideDeadband` — too wide and a ball clearly on one side is played by the other; too narrow and standing keep-ups stop alternating. If a ball down the middle is always the same foot, that is the planner's alternation, not a value |
| *(from S16/S17)* The foot reaches too far, like a string / falls short of a ball I should have had | The `BodyModel` reach for that limb. Too far is the string rendered — lower it; the planner must call it a miss. Falls short of a ball the landing ring put within a step: raise it. If the ball itself moved toward the foot, that is a bug, not a value |
| *(from S17)* The foot arrives late or early | Not a tuning value in the first place: the strike path is timed to the plan's contact time by construction. Late means the plan was not re-planned that step, or the strike path's lead time is shorter than the flight — a code finding |
| Pass charging never feels worth it | `MaxSpeed` too low, or `ChargeTime` too long |

### Two limits

**The tests are right and the value is wrong.**
`tests/LopFBBounce.Domain.Tests/Ball/BounceSolverTests.cs` encodes the intended
behaviour — among others, that a drifted ball is gathered back over several
touches rather than one, and that tapping at head level never buys speed. If a
tuning value breaks a domain test, do not change the test.

*(This paragraph used to cite "the ball matching its owner's speed to within 1%
after one second". That test was deleted by S8, and the behaviour it encoded —
a ball converging on its owner's velocity — is the ball following the player,
which is the thing S7 and S8 were written to destroy. A stale citation in the
one place this document appeals to authority.)*

**Know when it is not a tuning problem.** If no combination makes
running-while-juggling satisfying, that is a design failure rather than a tuning
one. Hand it to `game-designer`; the honest fix is GDD §3.2, not a fifty-first
iteration.
