One goal, one keeper. Keep the ball up, then finish in the air with a volley or a header. A ball that bounces before the line does not count, and a miss or a catch would send you in goal. Nine points and the keeper is out.

WASDRun. Shift to sprint. MouseLook and aim. The crosshair picks the spot on the goal. SpaceTouch the ball up. Foot, knee, chest or head, by height. ClickHold to charge, release to strike. The arc shows what you get. RDrop a new ball at your feet. BShow six players, to see what six bodies cost.
Needs a keyboard and mouse.
